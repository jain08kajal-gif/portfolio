About me

Since transitioning from UI design to product design, I've focused on building products that help businesses grow while creating experiences people genuinely enjoy using. I began my career as a visual designer, but curiosity about the why behind design decisions gradually shaped my approach to product thinking.

Over the years, I've adapted to diverse organisational cultures and collaborated with cross-functional teams to solve complex product challenges. My experience spans B2B SaaS and B2C products across logistics, live streaming, and e-commerce, where I've balanced user needs with business goals to create scalable, impactful solutions.

Outside of work, I find joy in simple things like spending time in nature, noticing thoughtful systems and appreciating everyday beauty. I believe that the best products are built in environments where people feel trusted, supported, and inspired because a healthy culture doesn't just improve the quality of work, it makes the work itself more meaningful.
